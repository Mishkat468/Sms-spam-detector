How it works

Each message is lowercased and split into words. Links, long numbers and currency symbols become special tokens, and common filler words are dropped. The model counts how often each word appears in spam and in normal messages, then multiplies those word probabilities (as log sums) with Laplace smoothing. The higher total wins.

The built-in dataset is a small hand-written sample in the style of the UCI SMS Spam Collection. To go further, swap in the full dataset and retrain.
